Ashta Chamma Rules

  • 2 players only.
  • Each player has 6 pawns.
  • Use two traditional Bara/Dayakattai sticks.
  • Dayam = 1
  • Rendu = 2
  • Mūdu = 3
  • Nālugu = 4
  • Ayidu = 5
  • Āru = 6
  • Bārā = 12
  • 1, 6 and 12 allow a pawn to enter.
  • Landing on an opponent pawn captures it unless the square is safe.
  • First player to take all 6 pawns home wins.
