Ashta Chamma Rules

  • 2 Players: Player 1 (Red) and Player 2 (Blue).
  • 6 Pawns per player starting in their yard.
  • Throws:
    • 1 = Dayam (Entry & Extra throw)
    • 2 = Rendu
    • 3 = Mūdu
    • 4 = Nālugu
    • 5 = Ayidu
    • 6 = Āru (Entry & Extra throw)
    • 12 = Bārā (Entry & Extra throw)
  • Capturing: Landing on an opponent pawn on any normal cell captures it back to their yard.
  • Safe Squares (★): Marked cells prevent captures. Multiple pawns can rest safely together.
  • Winning: First player to bring all 6 pawns to center Home wins!
