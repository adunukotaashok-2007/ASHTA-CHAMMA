📜 Ashta Chamma Rules

🎯 Sticks (Dayakattai)

Two traditional sticks with faces: blank, 1 pip, 2 pips, 3 pips.

Value = sum of both stick faces. Both blank = BĀRĀ = 12.

🔢 Throw Values

Dāyam1✅ Entry + Extra Turn
Rendu2❌ No entry, no extra
Mūḍu3❌ No entry, no extra
Nālugu4❌ No entry, no extra
Ayidu5❌ No entry, no extra
Āru6✅ Entry + Extra Turn
Bārā12✅ Entry + Extra Turn

🛡️ Safe Squares

The 8 crossed (X) squares are safe. No captures there. Multiple pawns can share.

⚔️ Capturing

Land on an enemy pawn on a non-safe square to send it back to their yard.

🔒 Special Inner Rule

A pawn can enter the inner ring / home ONLY after that player has captured at least one enemy pawn. Until then, pawns must stop at the end of the outer ring.

🏆 Winning

First player to get ALL 6 pawns into the centre home wins!
