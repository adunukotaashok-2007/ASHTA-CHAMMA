Ashta Chamma Rules

  • 2 Players, 6 pawns each
  • Throws: 1=Dayam, 2=Rendu, 3=Mūdu, 4=Nālugu, 5=Ayidu, 6=Āru, 12=Bārā
  • 1, 6, 12 = Entry & Extra Turn
  • Land on opponent to capture (unless ★ safe square)
  • First to bring all 6 pawns home wins!
