Ashta Chamma Rules

  • 2 Players — 6 pawns each
  • Sticks: Dayam(1), Rendu(2), Mūdu(3), Nālugu(4), Ayidu(5), Āru(6), Bārā(12)
  • Enter board only with 1, 6 or 12
  • 1, 6, 12 also give Extra Throw
  • Land on opponent → capture (except ★ safe)
  • First to get all 6 pawns to 🏠 Home wins
